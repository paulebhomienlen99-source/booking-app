## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How long will it take to go viral?**
A: Typically 4-8 weeks of consistent effort. Week 1 momentum is crucial.

**Q: Do I need a budget?**
A: No. Start with $0 (organic). Invest once you have traction.

**Q: What if my content doesn't resonate?**
A: Track what works. Double down on top performers. Iterate weekly.

**Q: Should I use all channels?**
A: No. Start with 3 (Twitter + 1 Reddit + Email). Add others after week 2.

**Q: How do I measure success?**
A: Track: Users, Bookings, Revenue, Traffic Source, Engagement Rate

**Q: What's the most important channel?**
A: In order: Referrals → Organic Search → Community → Paid Ads
