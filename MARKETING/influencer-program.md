# BookHub - Influencer Partnership Program

## Program Overview

BookHub is seeking influencers, content creators, and micro-celebrities to partner and promote our service booking platform.

## What We Offer

### Tier 1: Micro-Influencers (1K-10K followers)
- **Commission:** 10% of all referral bookings
- **Perks:** Free premium account, exclusive features
- **Support:** Marketing materials, demo account
- **Monthly Potential:** $500-$2,000+

### Tier 2: Mid-Tier Influencers (10K-100K followers)
- **Commission:** 12% of all referral bookings
- **Perks:** Premium account, affiliate link, exclusive features
- **Support:** Dedicated account manager, custom materials
- **Monthly Potential:** $2,000-$10,000+

### Tier 3: Macro-Influencers (100K+ followers)
- **Commission:** 15% of referral bookings
- **Perks:** Premium account, white-label features, exclusivity
- **Support:** Custom integrations, co-marketing campaigns
- **Sponsorship Deals:** Negotiable

**Earn Double:**
- 80% commission on bookings through BookHub

**How It Works:**
1. Join BookHub as a service provider
2. Get a referral link for other service providers
3. Earn commission on every provider they bring
4. Build your network and scale income

**Example:**
- You earn $80 on each booking you provide

- Passive income from your network
