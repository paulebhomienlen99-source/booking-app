HOW TO SHOOT THESE VIDEOS:
================================================================================

Equipment needed: Just your phone

For each video:
1. Open BookHub app on your phone
2. Open phone camera (or TikTok app)
3. Record video showing the screens/features
4. For text overlays: Use TikTok's built-in text editor
5. Add music from TikTok library (pick upbeat song)
6. Add hashtags
7. Post!

Tips:
- Hold phone steady (or prop it up)
- Good lighting (natural light best)
- Clear audio if voiceover
- 20-30 seconds per video
- Post 3 videos at same time (3-4 PM)

Time per video: 15-20 minutes

Total time: 45 minutes to 1 hour
